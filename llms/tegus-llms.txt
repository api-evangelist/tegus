# Tegus

> Tegus is an investment-research platform for institutional investors — an expert-call
> transcript library (260,000+ compliance-reviewed 1:1 expert transcripts across 27,000+
> public and private companies), company financial models and KPIs, SEC filings and an
> Excel add-in. AlphaSense completed its acquisition of Tegus on 8 July 2024 for ~$930M.
> Tegus now operates as a brand inside AlphaSense: tegus.com is a ten-page marketing and
> legal site that routes visitors to AlphaSense, the live application runs at
> app.tegus.co, and the developer surface has been retired.

## Status of the developer surface

- The Tegus developer hub at https://tegus.readme.io/ returns HTTP 302 to /inactive.
- status.tegus.com — which formerly carried a component named "Our Public API" — no
  longer resolves (NXDOMAIN). Last Internet Archive capture 2025-04-11.
- api.tegus.com, app.tegus.com, developer.tegus.com, docs.tegus.com, api.tegus.co,
  docs.tegus.co, developer.tegus.co and status.tegus.co all return NXDOMAIN.
- No OpenAPI, AsyncAPI, GraphQL SDL, MCP server, A2A agent card, llms.txt or
  security.txt is published at any tegus.com or tegus.co host.
- Programmatic access to the Tegus corpus is now delivered through the AlphaSense
  developer platform. See https://developer.alpha-sense.com/.
